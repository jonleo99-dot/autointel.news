A PRACTICAL WEBSITE CHECK

Three lenses.
One clearer picture.

A polished website can still leave buying steps unresolved. The scanner looks for public evidence across three parts of the experience.

01 / BE FOUND

AI discoverability

Can software find and interpret your dealership and vehicle information?

  • Inventory and vehicle pages
  • Structured vehicle details and pricing
  • Dealer identity and site discovery
02 / MOVE FORWARD

Transaction readiness

Are the tools for the next buying step visible on your public website?

  • Payments, trades, and finance
  • Deal building and reservations
  • Deposits, signing, and delivery paths
03 / REDUCE FRICTION

Buyer friction

Where could an interested shopper hit a barrier or lose the next step?

  • Price clarity and lead gates
  • Page health and workflow continuity
  • Clear next steps and human handoff

Evidence first. Each finding includes its status and confidence. Unknowns stay visible. Scans with insufficient evidence remain unscored.
