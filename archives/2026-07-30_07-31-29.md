    <span class='badge'>FEATURE STORY</span>
    <h2>Ford raises guidance for 2026 as F-Series production recovers</h2>
    <p>Despite headline Q2 weakness and EV-related charges, Ford lifted and narrowed its 2026 profit outlook to $10–$11B, signaling confidence that high-margin trucks and SUVs will carry the near term. Management cited healthier F-Series supply, record Bronco family performance, rapidly growing Maverick Hybrid sales, and richer mix trims (Raptor, Tremor) as margin levers while it restructures EV and battery JVs. Ford also teased a ~$30,000 next-gen electric truck on a new UEV platform targeting cost and quality breakthroughs, alongside expanding software subscriptions (now 1.6M paid, including 900K Ford Pro). The message: absorb today’s EV reset, monetize trucks and software now, and relaunch EVs where affordability and scale pencil.</p>
    <div class='hero-tension'><b>The Tension:</b> Profit today vs. reinvention tomorrow — Ford is leaning on gas and hybrid trucks to fund an EV reboot while convincing investors its low-cost EV plan will arrive fast enough.</div>
